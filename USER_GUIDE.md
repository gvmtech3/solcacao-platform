## What is SolCacao?

SolCacao is an online artisanal chocolate shop with two sides:

- **Shop** — for individual customers: single-origin bars, truffle boxes,
  seasonal collections, and a build-your-own sampler.
- **Wholesale** — for businesses that want custom-branded or bulk chocolate
  orders (corporate gifting, retail partners, events).

You pick one side when you create an account (or just browse both as a
guest before deciding).

## Switching between English and Spanish

Click the **EN / ES** toggle in the top navigation bar at any time. The
entire site — including menus, product descriptions, forms, and error
messages — switches language immediately. Your choice stays in effect for
the rest of your visit.

## Shopping as a guest (no account needed)

You do **not** need to create an account to browse, add items to your
cart, or check out. Guest checkout is fully supported:

1. Browse the **Shop** page and click any product to see its details.
2. Click **Add to Cart**.
3. Click the cart icon in the navbar to review your cart, then **Checkout**.
4. Fill in your shipping details and place the order — you'll get an order
   confirmation with no account required.

Your guest cart is remembered on your device for your current browser, so
it's still there if you navigate away and come back. If you later create
an account and log in, anything already in your guest cart is
automatically carried over into your account cart — you won't lose it.

## Browsing and filtering the catalog

On the **Shop** page you can:

- Filter by **category** (bars, truffles, seasonal), **origin**, **cacao
  percentage**, and **price range**.
- **Sort** results (e.g. price low to high).
- **Search** using the search box in the navbar.

Click any product tile to see its full description, origin story, cacao
percentage, and price before adding it to your cart.

## Building your own sampler

The **Sampler** page lets you hand-pick individual chocolate pieces (up to
9) to build a custom box:

1. Go to **Sampler**.
2. Click pieces to select them — your running total ("x / 9 pieces
   selected") updates as you go.
3. If there are more pieces than fit on one screen, use the pagination
   controls to see the next page — your selections carry across pages.
4. Once you're happy with your selection, add it to your cart like any
   other product.

## Creating an account

Click **Log In** in the navbar, then **Create Your Account**. You'll be
asked for your name, email, a password, and whether this is a **Retail**
(individual) or **Business** (wholesale) account — choose based on how
you'll mainly use the site, since this can't be changed later from your
account page.

**Important:** after you submit the registration form, you are **not**
automatically logged in. Instead, the form switches to the **Login** tab
with a confirmation message. This is expected behavior, not an error —
simply enter the email and password you just chose and click **Log In**.

If something in the form is invalid (for example, a name with numbers in
it, or a password that's too short), you'll see a specific message
explaining exactly what to fix, right on the form.

## Logging in and out

- **Log in:** click **Log In** in the navbar and enter your email and
  password.
- **Log out:** once logged in, use the account menu in the navbar and
  choose **Log Out**. You'll see a brief confirmation message.

If you enter the wrong email or password, you'll see a clear "invalid
email or password" message — your entry is never silently accepted.

## Checking out

1. Add items to your cart (as a guest or while logged in).
2. Click the cart icon, review your items and quantities, then
   **Checkout**.
3. Fill in your shipping address and contact details.
4. Review your order total (calculated securely by the store, not just
   your browser) and submit.
5. You'll land on an order confirmation page with your order details.

If a field is invalid — like an improperly formatted ZIP code — you'll see
an inline error pointing to exactly which field needs fixing, rather than
a generic failure message.

## Managing your account

Once logged in, click your name in the navbar to reach **My Account**:

- **Profile** — update your first name.
- **Order History** — see your past orders (available for **Retail**
  accounts; Business accounts place wholesale quote requests instead of
  retail orders, so this tab doesn't apply to them).

## Wholesale / business orders (B2B)

If you're ordering on behalf of a business, visit **Wholesale** in the
navbar (this is available to everyone, including guests — you don't need
an account to request a quote):

1. Go to **Wholesale** to read about available services (custom molds,
   luxury packaging, white-label, bulk gifting).
2. Click through to the **Custom Order** wizard, a guided 4-step form:
   1. Choose your cacao profile preferences.
   2. Optionally upload your brand assets (logo files — PDF, SVG, or AI,
      up to 10MB).
   3. Choose your packaging preference.
   4. Review everything and submit.
3. You'll receive a **reference quote number** and a confirmation page.
   Our team follows up using the contact details you provided.

**Note:** if you're logged in with a **Business** account, the site shows
you only the Wholesale area — the regular Shop, Sampler, and Cart are
hidden, since those are for individual retail purchases. If you need to
buy something as an individual too, use a separate Retail account.

## Contacting us

Visit the **Contact** page for general questions, wholesale inquiries, or
support requests. Fill in your name, email, and a message (at least 10
characters) and submit — you'll see a thank-you confirmation once it goes
through.

## Frequently asked questions

**I just registered — why can't I see my account as logged in?**
Registration and login are two separate steps by design. After
registering, log in on the tab the form switches you to, using the same
email and password.

**I'm a Business account — where did Shop/Sampler/Cart go?**
Those are retail-only sections. Business accounts see the Wholesale area
instead. If you try to navigate directly to a retail page while logged in
as a Business account, you'll be redirected back to Wholesale.

**Do I need an account to place an order?**
No — guest checkout is fully supported for retail purchases, and the
Wholesale quote wizard also works without an account.

**I added items to my cart before logging in — did I lose them?**
No. Your guest cart automatically merges into your account cart the
moment you log in.

**The site is only in English — can I switch it?**
Yes — use the **EN / ES** toggle in the navbar at any time.
